The Permanent Capital Index™ (PCI) follows the licensing model of established index
providers (MSCI, S&P Dow Jones, FTSE Russell): **the methodology is open and public** for
transparency and credibility, while the **brand** and the **official index data** are
protected. A methodology, as an idea, is not itself copyrightable; the value lies in
canonical status, brand, and reference data — which is exactly why the method
is published openly.
